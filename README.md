# Crochet by Chandu

### Little loops, lots of love ♡

Welcome to Crochet by Chandu, a small handmade crochet business creating cheerful, thoughtful pieces one stitch at a time. From everyday accessories to tiny keepsakes and cosy baby items, every creation is made with care and a little imagination.

## What you’ll find

- **Hair accessories** — crochet headbands, hair ties, bows, and more
- **Keychains and keepsakes** — colourful little companions for keys, bags, or gifts
- **Baby crochet** — small sweaters, headbands, gloves, socks, and other tiny pieces
- **More handmade creations** — a growing mix of crochet favourites and gift ideas

## Custom requests

Have a particular colour, style, or idea in mind? Share it with Chandu. Custom pieces are considered based on what’s feasible, and details can be discussed before an order is confirmed.
